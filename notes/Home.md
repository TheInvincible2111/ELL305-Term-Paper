## Workflow



1. Review the current notes before drafting so that later Obsidian changes are incorporated.
2. Consult `../sources/archbook.pdf` for the relevant ELL305 foundation, especially Chapters 9--12.
3. Capture source-specific claims in a paper or textbook note.
4. Include page numbers and a Zotero citation key.
5. Link the note from the outline.
6. Draft or revise a named LaTeX chapter only from the cited notes and checked sources.
7. After substantive LaTeX changes, compile the active milestone and record the verification result in its draft notes.

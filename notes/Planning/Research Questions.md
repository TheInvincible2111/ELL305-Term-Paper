# Research questions

## Main question

How do chiplet-based organization and 3D-stacked cache help modern processors address limitations in performance, scalability, memory latency, power, and die-level integration, and what new trade-offs do these techniques introduce?

## Evaluation pattern

For each architectural innovation, identify the bottleneck it addresses, the benefit it provides, and the new bottleneck, cost, or trade-off it introduces.

## Scope safeguards

- Treat AMD Zen as the principal case study, not the entire intellectual subject.
- Keep comparisons workload-, metric-, and time-specific.
- Use packaging and manufacturing evidence only where it explains an architectural decision.
- Do not claim that a source has been fully read unless its reading note records a complete pass.

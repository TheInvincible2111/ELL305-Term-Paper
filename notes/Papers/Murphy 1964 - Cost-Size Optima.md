# Use in draft-initial

Source of the yield model in Part III (`eq:yield`) and Appendix C. Read on 28 Oct 2026: p. 1539, Eqs. 7–11.
- Eq. 7: p = e^(−Da) for a device of susceptible area a with spot-defect density D.
- Eq. 8: average over a normalised distribution f(D) of chip defect densities.
- Eqs. 9–11 for three simplified distributions: delta (Poisson, Eq. 9), triangular (Eq. 10,
  ((1 − e^(−D0 a))/(D0 a))²), rectangular (Eq. 11, (1 − e^(−2 D0 a))/(2 D0 a)).
- Murphy: distribution functions 1 and 3 (Poisson and rectangular) are "almost certainly inappropriate"; he uses
  the triangular case (Eq. 10) for most results; the three differ little at high yields.
- **Dong & Xie's Eq. 15 is Murphy's rectangular Eq. 11.** The draft keeps it (to match Dong & Xie) and states
  Murphy's preference; at the example's yields the difference is under one percentage point.

Rest of the paper (cost-size optimisation) not yet read.

# Relevance

AMD primary presentation connecting chiplet scaling with advanced packaging. Slides 13--19 provide the most directly useful material for the first-generation 3D V-Cache case study: the 64MB L3 cache die, structural silicon, direct copper-to-copper hybrid bonding, through-silicon vias, interconnect pitch, and AMD's reported power, density, and prototype-performance comparisons.

# Evidence boundaries

- This is a corporate technical presentation, not an independently reviewed benchmark study.
- The interconnect-efficiency, interconnect-density, and power comparisons are based on AMD engineering analysis and must be attributed to AMD.
- The gaming chart compares a fixed-frequency Ryzen 9 5900X with a 12-core 3D-chiplet prototype; it should not be generalized beyond the disclosed games and configuration.
- The presentation is strong evidence for the disclosed stack organization, but detailed thermal behavior and independent workload sensitivity require additional sources.

## Half-page appendix rule

Keep the essential argument, evidence, and transitions in the main body. Move any self-contained explanation exceeding roughly half a page to Appendix C or a later appropriately lettered appendix, then give the reader a short main-text summary and a cross-reference. Likely candidates include extended theoretical background, lengthy derivations, detailed source-by-source discussions, experimental methodology details, and technical digressions. Appendix A remains the plagiarism report and Appendix B remains the reading-indices report.

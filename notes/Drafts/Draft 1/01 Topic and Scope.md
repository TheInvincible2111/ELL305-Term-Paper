# Topic and scope

## Working research question

How do chiplet-based organization and 3D-stacked cache help modern processors address limitations in performance, scalability, memory latency, power, and die-level integration, and what new trade-offs do these techniques introduce?

## Boundaries

- AMD Zen is the main case study, not the entire intellectual subject.
- The paper is about architecture and organization, not a product catalogue.
- Intel may be used selectively as a comparative case.
- Packaging and fabrication details are included only when they explain an architectural decision.
- The breadth must support a 150-page final paper without padding.

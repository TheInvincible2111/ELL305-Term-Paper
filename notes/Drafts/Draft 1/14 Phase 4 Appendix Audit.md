# Phase 4 appendix audit

The report was reviewed using the agreed interpretation of the half-page rule: retain the material needed to follow the central argument and milestone progress, but relocate a self-contained extended explanation when that explanation alone exceeds roughly half a page.

## Result

- Appendix A contains the plagiarism-check evidence.
- Appendix B contains the supplied readability indices.
- No existing self-contained technical explanation requires relocation.
- The preliminary technical survey remains in the body because it is a sequence of separate, short source-status paragraphs and constitutes required evidence of work completed as of the milestone date.
- The mapping diagram, progress table, and Gantt chart remain in the body because each directly supports a required Draft 1 item rather than serving as an extended digression.

Future additions must be checked individually. Background tutorials, long derivations, source-by-source technical digressions, and extended experimental details should be placed in separately numbered appendices when they cross the threshold.
